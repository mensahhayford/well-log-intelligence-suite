# Data Sources and Licences

## FORCE 2020 lithology prediction dataset
- Source: Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.4351156
- Content: 118 wells, Norwegian Sea. Well logs plus a lithology interpretation.
- Log data licence: Norwegian Licence for Open Government Data (NLOD 2.0), as stated on the Zenodo page.
- Interpretation (label) licence: NOT stated on the pages I checked. [Unverified]
- Competition repository: github.com/bolgebrygg/Force-2020-Machine-Learning-competition (README has no licence statement.)
- Citation: Bormann P., Aursand P., Dilib F., Dischington P., Manral S. 2020. FORCE Machine Learning Competition.

## How W1 uses it
- Data is downloaded by the user from the source. It is NOT stored in this repository.
- Results are for learning and screening. Data are from Norway, not Ghana.
- The app accepts user-uploaded LAS files.
